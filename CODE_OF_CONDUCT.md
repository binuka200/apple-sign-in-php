# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment for
everyone participating in this project, regardless of experience, identity, or
background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give others room to learn.
- Protect private information and never publish another person's credentials,
  tokens, or security-report details.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions will
consider context, severity, and whether harmful behavior is repeated.

For conduct that violates GitHub's policies or requires a private report, use
[GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Security vulnerabilities must instead follow the project's `SECURITY.md` policy.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents this project.
